# Google for Nonprofits — Program Research

Source: https://www.google.com/nonprofits/ (plus linked Google support pages), scraped 2026-09-03.

This document summarizes the publicly available information on Google for Nonprofits: the
application process, eligibility rules, benefits, timeline, required documentation, and support
contacts. Where the source pages didn't state an exact figure (e.g. dollar amounts), that is
noted explicitly rather than invented.

---

## 1. Full Application Process (Step by Step)

1. **Confirm eligibility first.** Before applying, check that your organization type and country
   are supported. Google explicitly says to "confirm your organization meets criteria for the
   countries where the program operates" — eligibility and available products vary by country, so
   check the country-specific list before starting.
2. **Start the request.** Go to `google.com/nonprofits` and click **"Get started"** (top right of
   the page).
3. **Complete the account request form.** Submit your organization's details (legal name,
   registration/charity number, country, mission, contact info) through the online form.
4. **Get verified by Goodstack.** Google outsources identity/eligibility verification to a
   third-party partner called **Goodstack**. After submitting the form:
   - Goodstack reviews the organization's legal and programmatic status.
   - Goodstack **may contact you for more information or documentation** to confirm both the
     organization's status and the requester's affiliation with it.
   - Monitor the email used on the application — including **spam/junk folders** — for messages
     from **verifications@mail.goodstack.org**.
5. **Wait for a decision.** Google's own program page states most requests are reviewed in
   **2–14 working days**; a linked Google support article on the Goodstack verification step
   states **"most requests are reviewed within 3–5 business days."** (The two figures come from
   different pages — treat 2–14 working days as the outer bound and 3–5 business days as the
   typical case.)
6. **If rejected, request re-review.** Google support documents a path titled "How do I request my
   rejected account to be re-reviewed by Goodstack?" — meaning a rejected application can be
   resubmitted/appealed rather than being a final dead end. Specific resubmission steps live on
   Goodstack's own help center, not on the Google page.
7. **Activate each product individually once approved.** Approval unlocks the Google for
   Nonprofits account, but each offering (Ad Grants, Workspace, YouTube Nonprofit Program, Maps
   Platform credits, Gemini, etc.) must be **separately activated** inside the Google for
   Nonprofits dashboard — approval is not automatic enrollment in everything.
8. **Use the onboarding resources.** After activation, Google provides how-to guides, product
   documentation, and training courses (including AI-for-nonprofits courses via Google Skills) to
   help the org roll out each product.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements

**To qualify, an organization must:**
- Be a **registered charitable/nonprofit organization in good standing** in one of the **180+
  eligible countries/geographies** Google supports.
- Meet the **full eligibility requirements specific to its country** (these vary by country;
  Google's site includes a country selector for the exact local rules).
- Successfully complete **verification through Goodstack**, Google's validation partner, which
  confirms both the organization's legitimacy and the applicant's affiliation with it.
- Agree to the **Google for Nonprofits Additional Terms of Service**.

**Explicitly NOT eligible:**
- **Government entities or organizations** ("not a governmental entity or organization").
- **Hospitals / healthcare organizations** — note: a charitable *foundation* affiliated with a
  hospital can still qualify even though the hospital itself cannot.
- **Schools, academic institutions, and universities** — note: a philanthropic/fundraising arm of
  a school can still qualify, but the institution itself should use **Google for Education**
  instead of Google for Nonprofits.

## 3. Benefits Provided

| Product | What it provides |
|---|---|
| **Google Workspace for Nonprofits** | Nonprofit-specific discounts on Business Standard, Business Plus, and Enterprise Workspace editions; collaboration tools (email, docs, meet, drive) plus AI features aimed at volunteer management and grant-application workflows. |
| **Google Ad Grants** | In-kind advertising on Google Search to raise awareness, reach new supporters, and drive donations/mission engagement. *(Google's program page didn't restate the dollar figure on this scrape, but the Ad Grants program is widely documented elsewhere as providing up to $10,000/month in free Search ad spend — verify the current figure on the dedicated Ad Grants page before quoting it to a client.)* |
| **YouTube Nonprofit Program** | Access to YouTube's nonprofit program features (e.g. donation/fundraising features on video content); requires separate activation from the main account. |
| **Google Maps Platform credits** | Free credits for Maps/location-based tools (e.g. embedding maps, geocoding) for approved nonprofits; requires separate activation. |
| **Gemini & Gemini Notebook (AI tools)** | AI assistance for generating marketing assets, data-driven fundraising ideas, and educational resources "in hours instead of days," plus automation of administrative tasks. |
| **Google Analytics** | Supports fundraising tracking and donor-allocation metrics. |

Note: every product above is opt-in and activated individually after the base Google for
Nonprofits account is approved — approval does not auto-enable all products.

## 4. Timeline and Approval Process

1. **Submission** — organization submits the account-request form via google.com/nonprofits.
2. **Goodstack review** — third-party verification of legal/charitable status and the applicant's
   affiliation with the org.
   - Typical stated timelines: **2–14 working days** (Google program page) or **3–5 business
     days** (Google support article on the verification step specifically). Treat 3–5 business
     days as the common case and 2–14 working days as the range including slower/edge cases.
   - Goodstack may pause the review to request additional documentation, which extends the
     timeline until it's supplied.
3. **Decision** — approval or rejection, communicated by email from
   `verifications@mail.goodstack.org` (check spam/junk).
4. **If rejected** — the org can request re-review via Goodstack rather than needing to start a
   brand-new application from scratch.
5. **If approved** — the org gains access to the Google for Nonprofits dashboard and must
   individually activate each product it wants to use.
6. **Ongoing** — Google support has a separate article on checking account status
   (support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/1614637) for orgs who want to track where their
   application stands after submission.

## 5. Required Documentation

The Google program page itself is light on specifics and defers to Goodstack, stating only that
Goodstack "may contact you for more information or documentation, so that they can verify your
organization." The linked Google support article on getting verified likewise defers to
Goodstack's own help center article, **"What documents does Goodstack require to verify my
organization's nonprofit status?"**, for the authoritative list.

Based on the process description, expect to provide:
- **Legal registration/charity documentation** proving nonprofit status in your country (e.g.
  registration number, government charity registry listing, or equivalent legal recognition
  document — exact accepted document types are country-specific and listed on Goodstack's site,
  not Google's).
- **Proof of your affiliation** with the organization (e.g. that you are an authorized
  representative, staff member, or officer able to request the account on the org's behalf).
- Whatever **additional information Goodstack specifically requests by email** during review —
  this is handled case-by-case, so applicants should monitor the verification email address
  closely rather than assuming a fixed document checklist.

**Action item:** before advising a client organization, pull the live document checklist directly
from Goodstack's help center (linked from support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/12016036) since
Google's own pages intentionally don't duplicate it and it can change independently of Google's
site.

## Caveats / Gaps in Public Page Content

- The two published review-time estimates (2–14 working days vs. 3–5 business days) come from
  different Google pages and weren't reconciled on either page — both are quoted above rather than
  picking one.
- Exact document checklists and country-by-country eligibility rules live on Goodstack's site and
  in Google's country-selector tool, not in the static text of google.com/nonprofits — these are
  dynamic/interactive elements that couldn't be fully enumerated from a single page fetch.
- Ad Grants' commonly cited $10,000/month advertising value is general industry knowledge about
  the program, not text pulled directly from this scrape of google.com/nonprofits — reconfirm on
  the dedicated Ad Grants page before quoting a figure externally.
