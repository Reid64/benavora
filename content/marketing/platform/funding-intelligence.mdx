Funding Intelligence is the first checkpoint after an opportunity is discovered. Rather than asking staff to read a notice of funding opportunity end to end before deciding whether it is worth pursuing, the system reads the notice, any linked appendices, and any published FAQ, and produces a criterion-by-criterion eligibility comparison against the organization's stored profile.

The output is not a single score. It is a list: for every eligibility requirement stated in the notice, the system reports whether the organization's current profile meets it, and if not, what specifically is missing. A requirement for 501(c)(3) status for at least three years, a requirement for prior experience administering a federal award of a certain size, a requirement for services in a specific geography: each is checked and reported individually, with the source sentence from the notice attached so staff can verify the read is accurate.

This matters because eligibility failures are often buried in an appendix or a supplemental FAQ document, not the headline notice text. A program officer skimming the summary page can miss a disqualifying requirement and spend a week on a draft before discovering the organization was never eligible. Funding Intelligence surfaces that requirement on day one, before any drafting time is committed.

The system also keeps a record of prior opportunities from the same funder that the organization has pursued, including whether they were awarded, declined, or withdrawn. That history sits next to the current opportunity so staff reviewing a new notice from a familiar funder can see what happened last time without searching through email or shared drives.

Nothing here is a gate. The system does not block an application because a criterion is flagged; it surfaces the flag so a person can weigh it. An organization missing one soft-preference criterion out of a dozen might reasonably decide to apply anyway. That judgment call belongs to staff, and Funding Intelligence is built to inform it rather than replace it.
