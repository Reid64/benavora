Grant funding does not work the same way across every kind of nonprofit. A faith-based organization draws on a different mix of denominational funds and community foundations than a housing developer drawing on HUD programs and low-income housing tax credit adjacent foundations. A veterans organization applies to a different set of federal, state, and legacy-organization sources than a school foundation applying to Department of Education programs and local giving.

Benavora's platform, described on the Platform pages, is the same underlying system regardless of organization type: discovery, eligibility scoring, drafting, portal submission, pipeline tracking, and analytics. What differs by organization type is which funding sources are relevant, which eligibility criteria come up repeatedly, and which application patterns are common.

The six pages linked below describe the funding problems specific to each organization type this platform currently supports, and which platform capabilities address each one. Faith-based organizations, human services providers, housing organizations, veterans organizations, education organizations, and community development organizations each have their own page.

These pages are not separate products. An organization identifies with the type that matches its work, and the underlying platform, its capabilities, and its human review checkpoints are the same six systems described on the Platform pages. The Solutions pages exist to make it faster to see how those systems map onto the specific funding landscape an organization already knows.

If your organization does not fit neatly into one of these six categories, the platform's discovery, eligibility, drafting, and pipeline systems are not restricted to them; the categories reflect the six org types this site currently documents in depth, not a limit on what the platform can be used for.
