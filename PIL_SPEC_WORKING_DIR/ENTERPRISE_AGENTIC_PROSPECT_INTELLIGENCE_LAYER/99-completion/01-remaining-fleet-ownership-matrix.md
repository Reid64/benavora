## Boundary law

All 30 specifications depend on deterministic services for mechanical execution. No browser, parser, queue, event bus, database, entity-match candidate generator, evidence store, model gateway, budget ledger, policy engine, CRM connector, or evaluation harness is promoted to agent status.

Cross-agent work uses typed delegation with authority intersection, maximum depth three, tenant isolation, budget inheritance, cancellation, deadlines, evidence requirements, and critic independence.
